Room rules

Quit Engine rooms · Effective 23 November 2026

Rooms are for focusing together. There is no chat: people see only a nickname, an avatar, what you focus on, your timer and your focus minutes. To keep rooms safe and kind, these rules apply to nicknames and room names.

Don't

  • Use rude, hateful, sexual or violent words, or attack anyone's religion, caste, gender, body or community.
  • Pretend to be someone else, a teacher, a coaching institute, or Quit Engine.
  • Add links, phone numbers, social-media handles or ads.
  • Share anything about another person, or try to find out who someone is.
  • Mention anyone's habits or health. Rooms are about focus, nothing else.

What happens

  • Anyone can report a nickname or a room name from the room. We review reports.
  • You can hide a person; you won't see them in any room on your phone.
  • We may remove nicknames or rooms that break these rules and block people from rooms, without notice.

Your data

Rooms use a random account with no email or name. You can delete your room profile at any time in the app (Rooms → your avatar → Delete my room profile). See the privacy policy.

Rooms are for people aged 13 and over.
