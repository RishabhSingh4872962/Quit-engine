## English (en-IN)

**App name** (24): `Quit Engine: Reels Limit`

**Short description** (79):
`Count your reels, set a daily limit and take back your time. Private by design.`

**Full description:**

```
Scrolled for "just five minutes" and lost an hour? Quit Engine helps you notice how many reels and Shorts you watch, set a limit that feels right, and slowly take your time back. No shaming, no lectures.

COUNT YOUR REELS
• Counts Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts as you watch, plus the time spent
• "Reels today" on your home screen, and an optional counter bubble while you scroll
• History for 7 or 30 days: per app, by hour of the day, late-night scrolling, and time won back

A DAILY LIMIT THAT HELPS
• Choose 10, 20, 30 or 50 reels a day
• Gentle notes at half and most of your limit
• At your limit, a calm breathing pause. Then you choose: stop, 5 more, or no more pauses today
• Your reel-free streak grows on every day you stay under your limit

BUILD THE HABIT
• Streaks, milestones and a home-screen widget that shows only a number
• "I'm having an urge" button with a short breathing exercise
• A slip doesn't erase your progress. Total days free keep counting
• Also track other habits: cigarettes, gutka or vaping, no contact after a breakup, nail biting, skin picking

PREMIUM
• Block reels in Instagram or YouTube while messages, feed and search keep working
• Reel-free hours for bedtime and study, per-app daily limits, and exam mode until a date you pick
• Strict mode, so rules can't be loosened in the moment
• YouTube study mode: only the channels you choose play, Shorts are blocked
• Reels screen time
The core app is free. Premium is a subscription you can cancel any time in Google Play.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your counts, streaks and screen time stay on your phone
• Notifications never say which habit you're working on
• No ads. Cloud backup is optional and stored in India
• English and Hindi

HOW REEL COUNTING WORKS
Quit Engine uses Android's Accessibility Service to count reels. It only works in Instagram and YouTube, and only after you agree on a screen that explains it. It notices when the reels player is open and when you swipe. It never reads your messages, comments or what is in the reels, and nothing it sees leaves your phone. It also powers the pause at your limit and the blocking you set up. You can turn it off any time in Android settings.

Quit Engine is a habit tracker and not a medical service. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a professional.
```
